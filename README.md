# Road Accident Analysis Dashboard

![Road Accident Dashboard](image/dashboard.png)
Executive Summary
This dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of road accident casualties, examining severity levels, vehicle types, road conditions, and temporal trends. The goal is to identify high-risk factors and support data-driven decisions for road safety improvements.
> Tools Used: Microsoft Excel, Power Query (Data Cleaning), Power Pivot & DAX (Analysis).

 Key Insights
### 1. Monthly Trends (CY vs PY)
- The line chart compares Current Year (2021) vs Previous Year (2020) casualties.
- Casualties generally increase from March to November, peaking in October/November.
- A notable drop occurs in December, likely due to reduced traffic during holidays.
### 2. Casualties by Road Type
- Single Carriageway is the most dangerous road type with 309,698 casualties.
- Dual Carriageway follows with 67,368 casualties.
- Slip Roads and One Way Streets have the lowest casualty counts.

### 3. Road Surface Conditions
- Dry roads account for the majority of casualties (279.4K), which correlates with higher traffic volume.
- Wet roads contribute 115.3K casualties.
- Snow/Ice conditions show the lowest numbers but represent the highest risk per incident.

### 4. Location/Area Analysis
- Urban areas: 255.9K casualties (higher density, more traffic).
- Rural areas: 162.0K casualties (higher speeds, less lighting).

### 5. Light Conditions
- Daylight: 305.0K casualties (majority due to higher traffic volume).
- Dark: 112.9K casualties (higher severity risk due to visibility issues).

## Strategic Recommendations
### 1. Road Safety Improvements
- Focus on Single Carriageways: Implement better signage, speed cameras, and road markings on single carriageways, as they account for ~74% of all casualties.
- Improve Rural Road Lighting: Since dark conditions increase severity, invest in street lighting for high-risk rural routes.

### 2. Seasonal Campaigns
- Autumn Safety Campaign: Launch targeted awareness campaigns in October/November when casualties peak.
- Winter Preparedness: Ensure rapid response for snow/ice conditions, as these incidents tend to be more severe.

### 3. Vehicle-Specific Measures
- Motorcycle Safety: With 33,672 casualties, motorcyclists are highly vulnerable. Promote protective gear and dedicated lanes.
- Car Safety Programs: Since cars represent 79.8% of casualties, focus on driver education and anti-collision technology.

### 4. Urban vs. Rural Strategies
- Urban: Focus on pedestrian safety and traffic flow optimization.
- Rural: Enforce speed limits and improve emergency response times.
